# LolaCooks: Down-the-Road Website Priorities

## Purpose

The current LolaCooks website establishes the brand, explains the three service levels, and gives potential clients a clear way to contact Lola. The next stage should gradually turn the website from a polished marketing page into a useful service platform.

The priorities below are ordered by practical value. They do not all need to happen at once.

## 1. Create a Client Inquiry and Onboarding Form

Replace the Instagram-only starting point with a short, focused inquiry form.

The form could collect:

- Name and contact information
- Service tier of interest
- Fitness or nutrition goals
- Preferred weekly meal quantity
- Dietary restrictions and allergies
- Pickup or delivery preference
- Ideal start date

Why this matters:

It would help Lola receive organized, qualified inquiries without repeating the same questions in every initial Instagram conversation.

## 2. Confirm Pricing, Policies, and Service Boundaries

Finalize and publish the operational details clients need before ordering:

- Prices for 8-, 10-, and 12-meal subscriptions
- Payment schedule
- Automatic-renewal terms
- Pause and cancellation deadlines
- Weekly ordering cutoff
- Pickup and delivery schedule
- Delivery area and delivery fees
- Dietary-accommodation policy
- Allergy and cross-contamination language

Why this matters:

Clear expectations reduce confusion, protect Lola’s time, and help clients make decisions confidently.

## 3. Add Stronger Trust Signals

Build confidence by showing more of the person and experience behind LolaCooks.

Possible additions include:

- A professional portrait of Lola
- A more personal founder story
- Client testimonials
- Client progress stories, shared with permission
- Nutrition-coaching education or credentials
- Behind-the-scenes preparation images
- Authentic meal photography

Why this matters:

Clients are not only purchasing meals. They are trusting Lola with their routines, goals, and nutrition support.

## 4. Add a Weekly Menu Page

Create a menu page that Lola can update without editing website code.

It could include:

- Current weekly meals
- Ingredients or short descriptions
- Macro information when available
- Dietary labels
- Ordering deadline
- Sold-out items

Why this matters:

A current menu gives returning visitors a practical reason to use the website every week.

## 5. Introduce Simple Customer and Subscription Tools

Once the ordering workflow is proven, consider adding:

- Secure online payment
- Subscription enrollment
- Pause or resume controls
- Weekly meal selection
- Order confirmation
- Customer order history
- Automated reminder emails or text messages

Why this matters:

These features could reduce administrative work, but they should be added only after Lola’s preferred operational process is clear.

## 6. Improve Local Search Visibility

Help nearby customers discover LolaCooks through search.

Potential improvements include:

- Location-specific website copy
- Clearly defined service areas
- LocalBusiness and service structured data
- A connected Google Business Profile
- Search-friendly page titles and descriptions
- Social-sharing images and metadata
- Consistent business information across platforms

Why this matters:

Local search can bring in customers who are actively looking for meal preparation or nutrition support nearby.

## 7. Add Legal and Expectation-Setting Content

Create clear language covering:

- Allergy and food-safety limitations
- Terms and conditions
- Privacy policy
- Refund and cancellation policy
- Nutrition-coaching scope
- A distinction between coaching and medical nutrition advice

Why this matters:

This protects both Lola and her clients while making the business feel established and trustworthy.

## 8. Continue Developing the Visual Brand

Build on the current visual system with more original brand assets:

- Professional food and lifestyle photography
- Custom service icons or illustrations
- Branded packaging photography
- Subtle food-inspired motion
- More behind-the-scenes imagery
- A consistent social-media visual system

Why this matters:

Original imagery and brand assets would move the website beyond a strong service site and make LolaCooks more memorable.

## Recommended Sequence

### Near term

1. Confirm pricing and policies.
2. Add the inquiry form.
3. Add testimonials, credentials, and original photography.

### Mid term

1. Launch an easy-to-update weekly menu.
2. Improve local search visibility.
3. Add legal and policy pages.

### Long term

1. Introduce online payments.
2. Add subscription and meal-selection tools.
3. Create a lightweight customer account experience.

## Recommended Next Move

The highest-value next feature is the client inquiry form. The website now explains the offers clearly; the next job is to capture qualified clients and give Lola the information needed to respond efficiently.
