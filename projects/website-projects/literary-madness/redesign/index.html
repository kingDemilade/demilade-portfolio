About this redesign

Literary Madness was inherited as a website redesign challenge in 2019. This new version reconstructs the surviving tournament and explores how the original image-based experience could become responsive, accessible, and maintainable.

The original campaign concept and artwork are preserved as reference material. This prototype does not claim current library affiliation, active voting, verified vote totals, or a confirmed tournament year.
