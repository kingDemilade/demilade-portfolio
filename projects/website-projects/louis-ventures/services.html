Services

Personalized guidance for every stage of your journey.

RL Pathways helps students, graduates, and professionals gain clarity, prepare for opportunities, and make confident decisions about education and career direction.

How Rose Can Help

Support that meets clients where they are.

Every client arrives with different questions, goals, and experiences. Services are designed to provide practical guidance, thoughtful support, and clear next steps based on each person’s needs.

01

Career Coaching

One-on-one guidance for clients who need help understanding their options, setting goals, and creating a realistic path forward.

  • Career exploration
  • Goal clarification
  • Professional development guidance
  • Personalized action planning
02

College & Education Planning

Support for students and families navigating academic options, college preparation, and long-term career alignment.

  • College pathway guidance
  • Major and career exploration
  • Academic planning support
  • Preparation for life after graduation
03

Resume & Interview Preparation

Practical support for clients preparing to apply for internships, jobs, programs, or new professional opportunities.

  • Resume review
  • Cover letter support
  • Interview preparation
  • Professional presentation guidance
04

Career Transition Support

Guidance for adults who are ready to make a career shift, return to the workforce, or explore a new professional direction.

  • Career change planning
  • Skill and experience review
  • Next-step strategy
  • Confidence-building support
