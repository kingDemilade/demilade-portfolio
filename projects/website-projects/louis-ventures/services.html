Frequently Asked Questions

Questions families may ask before booking.

Yes. School counselors can be stretched thin, while one-on-one guidance gives students focused support to maximize opportunities and reduce stress.

How do I know this will make a difference?

Each student receives a personalized plan with actionable steps for applications, scholarships, and clarity about what comes next.

Is this only for students applying to Ivy League schools?

No. LouisVentures supports students applying to competitive universities, local colleges, trade schools, and other career pathways.
