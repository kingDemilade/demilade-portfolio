# Website Redesign Proposal and Scope Agreement

Prepared for: Global Properties Realty, LLC  
Prepared by: Demilade Creatives  
Proposal date: June 30, 2026  
Proposal valid through: July 30, 2026

## Project Overview

Global Properties Realty needs a modern, mobile-friendly website that communicates its services clearly and gives clients a more reliable path to contact the brokerage and complete a rental application.

Demilade Creatives will redesign and launch the website, improve its content structure, and optimize the rental-application experience while continuing to use an established third-party provider for application fees, screening, consent, and sensitive applicant information.

## Included Services

- Project kickoff, content review, and website structure planning.
- Custom responsive website design for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Development of up to 10 core pages or equivalent page templates.
- Pages covering the brokerage, buying, selling, commercial real estate, REO properties, property management, rental applications, contact information, and privacy information.
- Clear call, text, email, inquiry, and rental-application actions.
- Light editing of existing website copy for clarity and consistency.
- Contact or inquiry forms with spam protection where supported.
- A redesigned rental-application landing page.
- Integration of the existing Formsite/Resident Research application or another mutually approved provider.
- A direct application link as a fallback when appropriate.
- Basic on-page SEO, analytics setup, accessibility checks, and performance optimization.
- Migration and launch on the approved hosting platform.
- Redirects for important existing pages where technically available.
- Two consolidated revision rounds.
- Thirty days of post-launch support for defects within the agreed scope.

## Rental Application Responsibilities

The website will direct or connect applicants to the client's approved third-party application provider. Demilade Creatives will not independently collect or store Social Security numbers, identification documents, payment-card information, background reports, or other sensitive screening data.

Global Properties Realty is responsible for approving application questions, fees, disclosures, privacy language, fair-housing compliance, screening criteria, vendor terms, and the final applicant workflow. The client will maintain its accounts and agreements with Formsite, Resident Research, payment processors, and any replacement providers.

## Not Included

- Custom applicant databases, tenant portals, payment systems, electronic signatures, or background-screening software.
- MLS/IDX feeds, CRM integrations, property-listing databases, or custom APIs.
- Logo or full brand-identity design.
- Professional photography, video production, or extensive copywriting.
- Legal, privacy, accessibility, regulatory, or fair-housing advice.
- Paid advertising, ongoing SEO, social-media management, or email marketing.
- Third-party fees, including hosting, domain registration, premium software, Formsite, Resident Research, payment processing, stock media, and licenses.
- Work requested outside the listed deliverables.

Additional services require a written change order describing the added fee and schedule.

## Schedule and Feedback

The estimated project duration is six to eight weeks, beginning after the agreement is signed and the initial payment, account access, and initial content have been received.

The client will provide one consolidated set of feedback within five business days of each review request. Delayed access, content, decisions, or feedback will extend the schedule. A project inactive because of missing client materials or feedback for more than 30 days may require rescheduling and a revised completion date.

## Revisions and Acceptance

Two consolidated revision rounds are included. Requests that change the approved direction, add pages or functionality, or expand the agreed scope will be estimated separately before work begins.

The client will inspect the website during final review and report material issues within five business days. Written approval, authorization to launch, or use of the completed website in production constitutes acceptance, subject to the included 30-day defect-support period.

## Ownership and Portfolio Use

After full payment, the client will own the final website-specific design and content created for this project, excluding third-party software, licensed assets, reusable development tools, frameworks, and pre-existing materials.

The client is responsible for maintaining valid licenses for third-party assets and services. Demilade Creatives may display the completed project in a portfolio, case study, proposal, or social channel unless the client requests confidentiality in writing before launch.

Payments already applied to completed work and reserved production time are non-refundable. After payment of all outstanding invoices, completed client-owned work will be delivered in its then-current state.
