Before work begins

A few things to confirm

It is okay if you do not know every answer. We can review these together.

1. Domain, website, and email

  • Do you own globalpropertiesrealty.com and have access to the account where it was registered?
  • Do you know which company currently hosts the website?
  • Do you have the Joomla administrator login or contact information for the current website manager?
  • Are any business email addresses connected to the domain?
  • Do you also own or use gprealtypropertymanagement.com?

2. Rental application and payments

  • Do you have a Formsite account and access to it?
  • Is Formsite owned by you, Resident Research, or another provider?
  • Do you have an active Resident Research account and a primary contact there?
  • Which service processes the application fee?
  • At what point are applicants experiencing difficulty?
  • Is there a way to test the application without charging a real applicant?

3. Business information and content

  • Do you have original logo files and photographs approved for website use?
  • What legal business, brokerage, license, and contact information must appear?
  • Do you have approved privacy, fair-housing, rental, or other required disclosures?
  • Which email address should receive website inquiries?
  • Who will provide final approval for website content?

4. Optional connections

  • Should the website display property listings through an MLS or IDX provider?
  • Do you use a CRM, Google Analytics, or Google Business Profile?
  • Which social-media and real-estate profiles should be linked?

MLS/IDX feeds, CRM integrations, custom property databases, and other new integrations require confirmation before they can be included or separately quoted.
