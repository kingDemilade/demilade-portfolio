# Global Properties Realty Website Redesign Scope and Pricing

Last updated: June 30, 2026

## Project Objective

Redesign the Global Properties Realty website into a trustworthy, responsive, and easy-to-maintain business website that supports buyers, sellers, property owners, tenants, and commercial clients. Improve the rental-application path while keeping sensitive screening and payment functions with an established third-party provider.

## Proposed Deliverables

### Strategy and Structure

- Project kickoff and requirements review.
- Review of existing content and user paths.
- Revised navigation and page hierarchy.
- Content organization for the brokerage's primary audiences.
- One visual direction based on the client's existing brand or approved updated direction.

### Website Design and Development

- Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Up to 10 core pages or equivalent page templates.
- Expected pages: Home, About, Buying, Selling, Commercial Real Estate, REO/Bank-Owned Properties, Property Management, Rental Application, Contact, and Privacy.
- Clear calls to call, text, email, request information, and begin an application.
- Updated header, navigation, footer, typography, colors, imagery, and content layout.
- Removal of irrelevant public CMS elements from the redesigned experience.
- Contact or inquiry forms with spam protection and confirmation states.
- Client-provided social links, contact information, brokerage details, and required disclosures.

### Rental Application Experience

- A new responsive rental-application introduction page.
- Clear instructions, estimated completion time, fee information, support contacts, and privacy context using client-approved language.
- Integration of the existing Formsite/Resident Research workflow or another client-approved provider.
- Mobile and desktop integration testing.
- Clear fallback link to open the application directly if an embedded form is unavailable.
- Confirmation and troubleshooting guidance where supported by the provider.

The third-party provider remains responsible for application processing, payments, electronic consent, background screening, and storage of sensitive applicant information.

### Content and SEO

- Light editing of existing copy for clarity, grammar, and consistency.
- Page titles and meta descriptions for core pages.
- Semantic heading structure and descriptive image alternative text.
- Local business contact and service-area information supplied by the client.
- XML sitemap, robots configuration, favicon, and social-sharing metadata where supported.
- Analytics installation using a client-owned account.

### Launch and Quality Assurance

- Browser testing in current versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge.
- Responsive checks at common mobile, tablet, and desktop sizes.
- Keyboard, focus, form-label, contrast, link, and image checks.
- Link and form testing.
- Basic performance optimization for supplied images and frontend assets.
- Deployment to the approved hosting provider and connection to the existing domain.
- Redirect mapping for important existing URLs where technically available.
- Thirty days of post-launch correction support for defects within the agreed scope.

## Client Responsibilities

The client will:

- Provide timely access to the domain, hosting, Joomla administration, analytics, Formsite, Resident Research, and related accounts.
- Supply accurate brokerage information, licensing details, required disclosures, privacy language, service descriptions, testimonials, photographs, and brand assets.
- Confirm that all supplied content and images may legally be used.
- Review deliverables and provide consolidated feedback within five business days.
- Test and approve the rental application, fees, consent language, screening process, and final production website.
- Pay all third-party subscription, transaction, hosting, domain, and licensing fees directly.

## Exclusions

The fixed project fee does not include:

- A custom applicant database or client portal.
- Custom payment processing, background checks, credit checks, or electronic signatures.
- Storage or transmission of Social Security numbers, financial records, identification documents, or screening results by the new website.
- MLS/IDX integration, property-search feeds, CRM automation, or custom API development.
- Logo design, full brand identity, professional photography, video production, or extensive copywriting.
- Legal, fair-housing, privacy, accessibility, or regulatory advice.
- Paid advertising, ongoing SEO campaigns, social-media management, or email campaigns.
- Unlimited pages, revisions, meetings, or content entry.
- Repairs to unrelated third-party services or pre-existing account problems.

Excluded work may be added through a written change order with additional pricing and timeline.

## Revisions and Change Requests

The project includes two consolidated revision rounds:

1. One revision round after presentation of the primary design direction.
2. One revision round after the functional website review.

Corrections that bring the work into the agreed scope are not counted as new features. New pages, integrations, redesign directions, or requirements introduced after approval require a change order.

## Pricing

Standard estimated project value: $5,000

Professional relationship and first-project discount: -$2,500

**Fixed project fee: $2,500**

Suggested payment schedule:

- 50% deposit to schedule and begin work: $1,250
- 25% after design approval: $625
- 25% after final approval and before production launch: $625

Deposits and milestone payments reserve production time and compensate work already performed. Third-party expenses are not included.

## Estimated Schedule

Estimated duration: six to eight weeks after receipt of the initial payment, signed agreement, required access, and initial content.

The timeline depends on feedback, account access, content delivery, and third-party provider responsiveness. Delays in client materials or approvals will move the delivery schedule.

## Optional Maintenance

Suggested maintenance plan: **$125 per month**, beginning after the included 30-day post-launch period.

The plan may include:

- Up to one hour of minor content updates per month.
- Website availability, form, and link checks.
- Dependency, CMS, or security updates where applicable.
- Basic backups where supported by the hosting platform.
- Monthly confirmation that the rental-application link or embed loads.

Unused time does not roll over. New pages, redesigns, integrations, emergency recovery, malware cleanup, and third-party service repairs are quoted separately. Maintenance terms should be confirmed in a separate agreement before recurring service begins.
