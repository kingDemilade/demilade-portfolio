## Purpose

This internal record summarizes the early review and pricing discussion for the proposed Global Properties Realty website redesign. It is not a client contract.

## Initial Website Assessment

The existing website is operational, but it needs more than a visual refresh. Observed concerns include:

- Dated Joomla presentation and inconsistent page hierarchy.
- The homepage presents selling content instead of a clear overview of the brokerage.
- Public-facing CMS elements such as a login form, search field, breadcrumbs, article details, and hit counters create confusion.
- Visible spelling, grammar, and content-quality issues weaken trust.
- Calls to action and paths for buyers, sellers, owners, tenants, and commercial clients are not clearly prioritized.
- The site needs stronger mobile behavior, accessibility, local SEO, analytics, and conversion tracking.

## Rental Application Assessment

The rental application is provided through an embedded third-party Formsite workflow associated with Resident Research, LLC. It includes application fees, applicant consent, background-screening language, and a multi-step submission process.

The embedded form uses a fixed-width layout of approximately 820 pixels. This can produce clipping, horizontal overflow, or an awkward experience on mobile devices. Because the form handles sensitive applicant information, fees, consent, and screening, the redesign should not recreate this functionality casually inside the website.

Recommended approach:

- Keep Formsite, Resident Research, or another established provider responsible for sensitive application data, payment, consent, and background screening.
- Build a clear, responsive rental-application landing page around the provider.
- Repair or replace the embed method when provider settings allow.
- Test the complete workflow on desktop and mobile without submitting a real paid application unless the client supplies a test procedure.
- Confirm who owns and administers the Formsite and Resident Research accounts before launch.

## Pricing Discussion

The estimated market value for the identified redesign is approximately $4,500 to $6,500, depending on content, integrations, migration requirements, and the final application workflow.

Demilade chose to offer a fixed relationship and portfolio rate of $2,500 because:

- This is a first paid client website project.
- The client has an existing professional relationship with Demilade.
- The client previously extended professional consideration to Demilade.
- The project can become a strong portfolio and referral opportunity.

The reduced fee is an intentional discount, not the standard value of comparable future work.

## Agreed Pricing Direction

- Fixed project fee: $2,500
- Suggested payment schedule: 50% to begin, 25% after design approval, and 25% before launch
- Suggested maintenance: $125 per month under a separate maintenance agreement
- Client-paid expenses: domain, hosting, premium software, Formsite, Resident Research, payment processing, stock assets, and other third-party services

## Scope Boundary

The $2,500 fee does not include building a custom rental-screening platform, storing sensitive applicant records, processing background checks, creating an electronic-signature system, or developing a custom payment system.

If the client requests any of those capabilities, the work must be separately scoped, priced, and reviewed for privacy, security, legal, and compliance requirements.
