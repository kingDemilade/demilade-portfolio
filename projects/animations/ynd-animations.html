Your Neighborhood Dashboard

Role: Motion & Visual Design

Design Approach & Concept

The Your Neighborhood Dashboard was designed to make location-based data clear and easy to explore. The approach focuses on clarity and structure, allowing users to quickly understand district-specific information tied to their address. The concept centers on local discovery, helping residents better connect with their immediate surroundings.

Execution

Motion was used to guide attention and support how information is revealed. Subtle transitions and data animations create a smooth, intuitive experience without overwhelming the user. The result is a cohesive system that enhances usability while making neighborhood data more engaging and accessible.
