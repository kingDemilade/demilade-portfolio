Palm Beach County Day 2026

Role: Motion & Visual Design

This project focused on creating a modular animation system that highlights the different sponsorship levels for Palm Beach County Day 2026. With the arrival of a new County Administrator, the theme “Charting Our Course” felt especially meaningful — inspired by the idea that, like navigating at sea, progress requires direction, teamwork, and momentum.

The nautical approach allowed each sponsor tier to feel distinct while still connected to a unified visual system. The animations were designed to live across screens and moments throughout the event, supporting sponsor recognition, reinforcing the theme, and reflecting the core message behind the day: Together, we sail forward.
